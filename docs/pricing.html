Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch plans at any time? ▾
Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When upgrading, you'll be prorated for the remainder of your billing cycle. Downgrade changes take effect at the start of your next billing period.
Is there a free trial available? ▾
We offer a 14-day free trial of the Biotech plan with no credit card required. This gives you full access to explore the platform's capabilities before committing.
What kind of support is included? ▾
Researcher plans include community forum support. Biotech plans get priority email support with 24-hour response times. Pharma plans include a dedicated account manager. Enterprise plans get 24/7 premium support with guaranteed response times.
How is data security handled? ▾
PharmaSpark is SOC 2 Type II certified. All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). Pharma and Enterprise plans add SSO, RBAC, audit logging, and the option for on-premise or air-gapped deployment.
Can I use PharmaSpark for commercial drug development? ▾
Absolutely. All plans allow commercial use. The Biotech plan is suitable for small commercial teams, while Pharma and Enterprise plans are designed for full-scale commercial pharmaceutical operations with compliance requirements.
Do you offer academic discounts? ▾
Yes, we offer a 40% discount on all plans for verified academic institutions and non-profit research organizations. Contact our sales team with your institution credentials to apply.
