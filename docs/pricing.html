Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about PharmaSpark pricing and plans

Can I switch plans mid-contract?

Yes. You can upgrade your plan at any time and the price difference will be prorated. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle. Annual plans include a 30-day satisfaction guarantee.

Is there a free trial available?

We offer a 14-day free trial of the Biotech plan with full feature access. Academic researchers may qualify for discounted pricing through our Academic Access Program — contact us for details.

What about data security and IP protection?

All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). Enterprise plans offer private cloud deployment, VPC peering, and data residency guarantees. Your compound data and models remain your intellectual property at all times.

Do you support on-premise deployment?

Yes, the Pharma Enterprise plan supports fully on-premise deployment on your own infrastructure. We provide Docker containers, Kubernetes Helm charts, and air-gapped installation options for high-security environments.

What regulatory compliance do you support?

PharmaSpark's GxP environment (Enterprise plan) is validated per FDA 21 CFR Part 11, EU Annex 11, and GAMP 5 guidelines. We provide IQ/OQ/PQ documentation and ongoing change control for validated workflows.

How does custom model training work?

Enterprise customers can train proprietary models on their internal data using federated learning — the model architecture trains on your data within your environment, and only model gradients (not raw data) are aggregated. This ensures full IP protection while leveraging PharmaSpark's ML infrastructure.

Need a Custom Solution?

For organizations with unique requirements — multi-site deployments, specialized therapeutic area models, custom integrations, or volume licensing — our enterprise team will design a tailored package.
