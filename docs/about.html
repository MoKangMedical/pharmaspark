Bridging the gap between computational drug design and clinical reality through AI-powered molecular intelligence

Our Mission

Drug development is one of the most expensive and failure-prone endeavors in science. The average new drug takes 10-15 years and costs $2.6 billion to reach patients. Despite advances in computational chemistry and AI, the translational gap between molecular design and clinical success remains vast.

PharmaSpark was built to close this gap. We believe that by integrating real-time 3D molecular visualization with AI-driven prediction across the entire drug development pipeline — from target discovery to regulatory submission — we can fundamentally change how therapies are developed.

Our platform combines cutting-edge 3D Gaussian Splatting technology for molecular visualization with state-of-the-art machine learning models trained on decades of pharmaceutical data, creating an end-to-end intelligence layer that accelerates every decision in the pipeline.

3D Gaussian Splatting for Molecular Science

Traditional molecular visualization relies on polygon-based rendering (ball-and-stick, space-filling models) that struggle with the complexity of large biomolecular systems. PharmaSpark pioneers the application of 3D Gaussian Splatting — a technique originally developed for photorealistic scene reconstruction — to pharmaceutical molecular visualization.

By representing atoms as 3D Gaussian primitives with physically meaningful radii, colors, and opacity values derived from atomic properties, PharmaSpark achieves real-time rendering of molecules with millions of atoms while preserving scientific accuracy. This enables researchers to explore protein-drug interactions, binding pocket dynamics, and molecular surfaces at unprecedented speed and visual fidelity.

The technology integrates directly with our PDB/SDF parsers, converting molecular coordinate data into optimized Gaussian splat representations that can be rendered, manipulated, and exported as standard PLY files for downstream analysis.

Our Journey

Key milestones in PharmaSpark's evolution

2024 Q1

Concept & Research

Initial research into applying 3D Gaussian Splatting to molecular visualization. Proof-of-concept demonstrating real-time rendering of protein structures with 100K+ atoms.

2024 Q3

Core Engine Development

Release of the TypeScript library with PDB/SDF parsers, atom-to-splat conversion, and Three.js renderer. First successful rendering of the full SARS-CoV-2 spike protein.

2025 Q1

AI Integration

Integration of target discovery, ADMET prediction, and binding affinity models. Knowledge graph connecting 2.5M biomedical entities from 35M publications.

2025 Q3

Platform Launch

Full platform launch with clinical trial simulator, regulatory intelligence, and HEOR modules. Onboarding of first 50 research teams across academic and biotech organizations.

2026

Enterprise & Scale

GxP-validated environment for enterprise customers. Federated learning for custom model training. Expansion to 200+ research teams with full drug pipeline management.
